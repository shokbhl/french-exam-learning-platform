# Product requirements

## Users and outcomes

Students learn French from A1 through C1/C2 and prepare for TEF Canada, TCF Canada, or both. Instructors assign work and provide feedback. Administrators manage versioned content, source materials, exam configurations, and user roles.

## Core journeys

- Onboard with level, target, schedule, exam date, strengths, weaknesses, and explanation language.
- Follow a daily study plan that blends general French with relevant exam tasks.
- Learn listening and reading with evidence, vocabulary, notes, and explanations, then practise under configurable exam constraints.
- Submit writing or speaking, preserve the original response, and receive rubric-structured feedback.
- Convert meaningful errors into a mistake notebook and spaced-repetition queue.
- Upload licensed private sources, review extracted drafts, and publish an approved immutable content version.

## Success criteria

The MVP is usable without AI, supports authenticated student/admin roles, provides original demo content for both exams, persists attempts and progress, protects private materials, and clearly labels all scores as unofficial practice estimates.
